Find ∫ x e2x dx.

My mistake

Picked u = e2x, which made the integral harder instead of easier.

Correct solution

Let u = x, dv = e2x dx, so the answer is ½x e2x − ¼e2x + C.

How to install

Pick your device. Each takes about two minutes.

Install on a Mac

Recall isn't from the App Store, so the first time you open it macOS asks you to confirm. That only happens once.

  1. Check your Mac has Apple silicon

    Click the Apple menu → About This Mac. If Chip says Apple M1 or newer (M2, M3, M4, M5…), you're good. If it says Processor: Intel, use the web version instead.

  2. Download Recall

    Click Download for Mac. You'll get Recall-mac.zip (about 110 MB) in your Downloads folder.

  3. Unzip it

    Open Downloads in Finder and double-click Recall-mac.zip. The Recall app appears next to it.

  4. Move it to Applications

    Drag Recall onto Applications in the Finder sidebar. (Running it from Downloads works too, but Applications keeps it tidy and in Launchpad.)

  5. Open it once

    Double-click Recall in Applications. macOS shows a message like “Recall” Not Opened or “Apple could not verify Recall is free of malware”. That's normal for apps from outside the App Store. Click Done (not Move to Bin).

  6. Allow it in Privacy & Security

    Open System Settings → Privacy & Security and scroll down to the Security section. Next to “Recall was blocked to protect your Mac”, click Open Anyway, then confirm with your password or Touch ID and click Open Anyway once more.

    On older macOS (Ventura or earlier) you can instead right-click Recall → Open → Open.

  7. Keep it in your Dock

    While Recall is open, right-click its Dock icon → Options → Keep in Dock. From now on it opens like any other app.

“Recall is damaged and can't be opened”

macOS sometimes says this about downloaded apps that aren't from the App Store. The app is fine. Open Terminal (⌘ Space, type Terminal), paste this and press Return, then open Recall again:

xattr -cr /Applications/Recall.app
Where are my cards kept?

In a folder called Recall in your home folder (Finder → Go → Home). In the app, File → Show Library in Finder opens it. Turn on sync to also keep a copy online.

How do updates work?

Recall checks for updates by itself. When one is ready, a bar says “A new version of Recall is ready”. Click Restart now. You can also use Recall → Check for Updates… in the menu bar.

Install on Windows

Recall isn't from the Microsoft Store, so Windows asks you to confirm the first time. That only happens once.

  1. Download Recall

    Click Download for Windows. You'll get Recall-windows.zip (about 115 MB) in your Downloads folder.

  2. Extract the zip

    Open File Explorer → Downloads, right-click Recall-windows.zip → Extract All… → Extract.

    Don't run Recall from inside the zip without extracting it first. It won't be able to save your cards.

  3. Put the folder somewhere permanent

    Move the extracted Recall folder somewhere it can stay, like Documents. Keep everything inside the folder together.

  4. Open Recall.exe

    Open the folder and double-click Recall (Recall.exe).

  5. Get past SmartScreen

    If a blue box says Windows protected your PC, click More info, then Run anyway. This only appears the first time.

  6. Make a shortcut

    While Recall is open, right-click its icon on the taskbar → Pin to taskbar. For a desktop shortcut, right-click Recall.exe → Show more options → Send to → Desktop (create shortcut).

My antivirus blocked it

Some antivirus programs are wary of new apps they haven't seen before. Choose Allow or Restore for Recall in your antivirus, then open Recall.exe again.

Where are my cards kept?

In a Recall folder inside your user folder (C:\Users\you\Recall). In the app, File → Show Library in File Explorer opens it. Turn on sync to also keep a copy online.

How do updates work?

Recall checks for updates by itself. When one is ready, a bar says “A new version of Recall is ready”. Click Restart now. You can also go to Themes → Updates → Check for updates.

Add to iPad or iPhone

There's no App Store download. Recall runs from its website and, once it's on your home screen, opens full screen like any app and keeps working offline.

  1. Open Recall in Safari

    On your iPad or iPhone, open Safari and go to whisz12.github.io/Recall.

  2. Tap the Share button

    It's the square with an arrow pointing up ⬆︎. On iPad it's at the top right; on iPhone it's at the bottom (or behind the ⋯ button on newer iOS).

  3. Tap “Add to Home Screen”

    Scroll down the list of actions to find Add to Home Screen (on newer iOS you may need to tap View More first). If you see Open as Web App, leave it switched on. Then tap Add.

  4. Open it from your home screen

    Always open Recall with the new Recall icon rather than from Safari. It opens full screen, without Safari's address bar, and works with no internet.

  5. Turn on sync

    Tap Turn on sync at the bottom of the sidebar and sign in (see Sync below). This keeps your cards safe and matches them with your computer.

    Do this on iPad and iPhone. If your device ever clears website data, synced cards come straight back when you sign in.
Using Apple Pencil

While studying, tap Scratchpad (or press P on a keyboard) to work out answers by hand. You get a stroke eraser, scribble-to-erase, and a full-screen mode with the question beside your working. Double-tap the page with a finger to swap between pen and eraser.

How do updates work?

Automatically. Each time you open Recall online it loads the newest version. If it has been open for a while, a bar offers Reload when there's an update.

Add to an Android phone or tablet

Recall installs straight from its website in Chrome. No Play Store needed.

  1. Open Recall in Chrome

    Go to whisz12.github.io/Recall in Chrome.

  2. Install it

    Tap the ⋮ menu at the top right → Add to Home screen (or Install app) → Install.

  3. Open it from your home screen

    Recall gets its own icon, opens full screen and works offline.

  4. Turn on sync

    Tap Turn on sync in the sidebar and sign in to match your cards with your other devices.

Chromebooks, Linux, Intel Macs and school computers

Anywhere with a modern browser can run Recall, with nothing to install.

  1. Open Recall

    Go to whisz12.github.io/Recall in Chrome, Edge, Safari or Firefox.

  2. Install it (optional)

    In Chrome or Edge, click the install icon at the right of the address bar (a small screen with an arrow) → Install. Recall then gets its own window and app icon.

  3. Turn on sync

    Click Turn on sync in the sidebar and sign in. On a shared or school computer this matters: your cards live in your account, not on that computer.

Sync

Sign in with the same email on each device and your cards, progress, images and theme stay matched. You can study offline and it catches up when you reconnect.

  1. Turn on sync

    In Recall, click Turn on sync at the bottom of the sidebar.

  2. Create an account on your first device

    Enter your email and a password (6 or more characters), then click Create account. Cards already in the app are uploaded.

  3. Sign in on the others

    Use the same email and password and click Sign in. Your cards appear within a few seconds.

Each person has their own private account. Nobody else can see your cards.
