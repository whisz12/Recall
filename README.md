## What it does

- **Mistake cards:** the question (text and screenshots), what went wrong, and the correct solution, with maths and rich notes.
- **Spaced repetition:** rate each card Again, Hard, Good or Easy and it comes back just before you'd forget it.
- **Exam countdowns and timed mode,** so everything comes up before exam day.
- **Scratchpad** for working by hand, with Apple Pencil support and a full-screen mode.
- **Hide and reveal** parts of diagrams to test yourself.
- **Import** from Anki and Quizlet, and share decks as `.recall` files.
- **Sync:** click **Turn on sync** and sign in on each device to keep one library everywhere.

## Updates

Recall updates itself on every device. The desktop apps download new versions in the background and offer a restart; the iPad, phone and browser versions load the newest version whenever they open.
